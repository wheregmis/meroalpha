What MeroAlpha is

MeroAlpha is a macOS app in which an AI agent researches the Nepal stock market and runs a paper account in virtual NPR. It places no real orders, holds no money and connects to no broker, TMS or demat account. Paper fills are a simulation built from observed trade prints; real orders can fill at other prices, or not at all.
